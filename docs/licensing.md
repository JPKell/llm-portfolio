# Licensing and Publication Boundaries

The MIT license covers learner-authored code, documentation, schemas, and synthetic fixtures in this repository.

It does not automatically cover:

- model/tokenizer weights or configurations;
- datasets or benchmark records;
- public-code training records;
- upstream utilities and research implementations;
- derived checkpoints/adapters/quantizations; or
- output that reproduces copyrighted or private material.

For every external artifact, preserve its source, immutable revision, license/terms, notice/attribution, intended use, redistribution decision, and removal path. Public availability is not permission. Exclude ambiguous material from the first public release.

This engineering checklist is not legal advice.
