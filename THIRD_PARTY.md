# Third-Party Artifact Boundary

No third-party source code, model weights, datasets, or benchmark records are vendored in the initial scaffold.

Future experiment manifests may reference Qwen models, TinyStories, llama.cpp, lm-evaluation-harness, PEFT, Transformers, SparseGPT, Wanda, torchao, and public-code sources. Every referenced artifact retains its own license, terms, notices, attribution, and redistribution policy. A reference in a manifest does not place that artifact under this repository's MIT license.

Before adding a dependency or redistributed sample, record its exact version/revision, source, SPDX expression where appropriate, license/NOTICE evidence, intended use, and publication decision.
